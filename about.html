OUR HERITAGE & MANUFACTURING SCALE

A 4th-Generation Legacy of Craftsmanship, Precision & Trust

Established in 1997, Ahmed Wood Group has evolved into a powerhouse enterprise combining multi-generational timber mastery with state-of-the-art European pressing and finishing technologies.

From our manufacturing origin in Saidpur, Nilphamari District, through our premier Middle Eastern procurement hub in Dubai (Alahmed Elite Venture Co LLC), we operate ultra-high-capacity production facilities spanning over 450,000 square meters.

Absolute commitment to innovation and rigorous quality control has driven our continuous investment in automated hydraulic pressing lines, precision sanding calibration, and resin chemistry research. Today, the AHMED brand sets the benchmark for reliability, structural integrity, acoustic performance, and design versatility across commercial hospitality, government landmarks, and high-end residential fit-outs.
