HERITAGE & INFRASTRUCTURE

Our Story

Our story starts in 1997, when Ahmed Wood Group first opened its doors in Saidpur, Bangladesh. What began as a plywood manufacturer has grown into a fourth-generation family enterprise, one that now runs production facilities spanning over 450,000 m² and supplies wood-based materials to markets around the world.

Al Ahmed UAE (Alahmed Elite Venture Co LLC) is the Group's dedicated sales and distribution arm for the UAE, KSA, and wider GCC region. Every panel, board, and laminate we sell is engineered and manufactured at the Group's Bangladesh facility, then held in a fully stocked warehouse right here in the UAE , so architects and contractors get the reliability of large-scale manufacturing with the speed of local stock, without the wait times or minimum order volumes that come with importing directly.

PRODUCT SPECTRUM

What We Make

Our range covers just about every application architects and contractors ask for — plywood, melamine faced chipboard, particle board, veneer board, acrylic board, and high-pressure laminate (HPL), alongside decorative plywood, WPC and PVC ceiling systems, edge banding, and acoustic wall panels.

Whether it's furniture, marine fit-outs, wall and roof systems, transport interiors, or packaging, there's an Ahmed panel built for it.

SURFACE INNOVATION

Innovation Spotlight: Ahmed HPL

Our latest innovation, Ahmed HPL, was built for architects and interior designers who need more than just a surface — they need one that performs. It comes in a wide range of colours, textures, and finishes, and holds up against scratches, heat, and stains without compromising on looks.

From wall cladding and countertops to bespoke furniture, Ahmed HPL is designed to turn a design vision into something that actually lasts on site.
